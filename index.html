Help & FAQ

How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat fee, and free on larger orders.

Which payment methods can I use?

Cash on Delivery, GCash and Credit/Debit Card are available at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Choose Track Order at the top of the page and enter your order number and email. Signed-in customers can also see all their orders in My Account.

How do coupons work?

New accounts get a welcome coupon automatically. Enter any coupon code in your bag or at checkout while signed in. One coupon can be used per order.

Can I return a product?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Please keep your order number handy.
